## What is Substrate?

**Agent Substrate** is a Kubernetes-native runtime for highly-multiplexed actor
workloads — AI agents, sandboxed environments, stateful services. It decouples
actor lifecycle from Pods, so a small pool of pre-warmed gVisor workers can host
**30× more actors than there are pods**, by suspending idle actors to object
storage and restoring them on demand.

## How to read this atlas

- **Mermaid diagrams** are the lingua franca: sequence diagrams for flows, state
  diagrams for lifecycles, flowcharts for topology.
- **Boxes are clickable** in the hero topology above and in every Mermaid
  diagram on the deeper pages — try clicking `ateapi` above to drill into its
  internals.
- **Code references** look like <code class="fileref">cmd/ateapi/main.go:97</code> —
  open those in your editor to confirm any claim made here.
